Small status pill used in tables — wishlist status, match state.

```jsx
<Badge tone="new">New</Badge>
<Badge tone="done">Handled</Badge>
<Badge tone="active">active</Badge>
<Badge tone="fulfilled">fulfilled</Badge>
<Badge tone="cancelled">cancelled</Badge>
```

`new`/`done` are the two Matches-view states (unhandled vs handled). `active`/`fulfilled`/`cancelled` are the three Wishlist statuses.
